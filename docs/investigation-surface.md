# Investigation workspace

Mode: Operate

Audience: Internal Ombudsman or Principal Nodal Officer reviewing one bank's complaint evidence.

Job: Identify the process failures driving complaint volume, trace each aggregate to supporting tickets, and record a defensible human decision without changing the original record.

Primary action: Open a ranked root cause, inspect its evidence, then confirm, dismiss, or reclassify a suspected misclassification.

Proof: bounded customer excerpts, trigger phrases, model confidence and version, branch concentration, onset timeline, immutable raw tickets, and append-only review events. All bundled values are labeled synthetic.

Constraints: desktop-first and responsive; English only; WCAG 2.2 AA; single-user identity boundary; no production accuracy claim; no raw complaint text outside the analytical store.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The screen makes analytical lineage visible as a continuous evidence chain from ingestion to human review. It refuses the generic executive-dashboard arrangement of disconnected KPI cards.

OWN-WORLD: A daylight evidence desk with a deep teal operational shell, celadon work surface, lime completion signal, cobalt analytical selection, and restrained regulatory state colors.
