# Quality bar: evidence desk

- A first-time investigator can identify the leading cause, its urgency, volume, change, confidence, and evidence path without interpretation help.
- The evidence chain is a working system-status instrument, not decoration; every stage reports real record counts and freshness.
- Aggregate claims resolve to bounded ticket excerpts, trigger evidence, model version, and append-only human decisions.
- Desktop density matches a high-quality compliance operations product; small-screen summaries preserve decision-critical fields instead of shrinking the desktop table.
- Keyboard users can enter and leave the mobile rail and evidence drawer predictably, and every selected state is communicated semantically as well as visually.
- Synthetic values and inferred associations remain visibly separate from confirmed production evidence.
